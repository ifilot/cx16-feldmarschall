HOW TO PLAY

Deploy, advance, capture the flag

01

Deploy your army

Drag your forty pieces from the tray onto your four home rows, or press L to load one of ten classic deployments and R for a random one. Start lights up once every piece is placed.

02

Move & attack

Drag a piece one square; gold dots mark where it can go. Scouts run any distance. Drop onto an enemy to attack: both pieces turn face up and the higher rank wins.

03

Remember

Blue's survivors turn face down again. Miners defuse bombs, the spy defeats the marshal when it strikes first. Capture the flag, or leave Blue without a move, to win.
