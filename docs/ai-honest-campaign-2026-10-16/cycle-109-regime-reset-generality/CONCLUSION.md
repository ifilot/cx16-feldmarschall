# One-time pursuit reset: honest development endpoint

Discarding old goals when public evidence rejects every opening template did
**not establish an improvement** against the honest Missions opponent. The
candidate scored **50.52%** (48 wins, one draw, 47 losses), versus **51.56%** for
the unchanged Fortress-template control (49 wins, one draw, 46 losses). The
paired difference was **−1.04 percentage points**, with a 95% bootstrap interval
**[−3.13, 0.00]**.

Only **one of 48 paired scores changed**, and it worsened; 47 tied. This sparse
result does not establish a reliable regression or policy equivalence. Its
bootstrap endpoint at zero should not be interpreted as strong evidence of a
generality loss. All 192 attempts completed without engine errors, so missing
outcomes introduce no additional uncertainty in this fixed development sample.

Both arms used the same 48 paired corpus records, eight per family, with the
same physical armies and move seeds. The exposed-rear family changed by −6.25
points; all other family means were unchanged. Small family samples and sparse
changed outcomes cannot establish stable family-specific effects.

The [trajectory diagnostic](divergence.json) found two changed complete game
sequences out of 96; both first differing moves were by the candidate at an
identical preceding position. One changed sequence retained its outcome. Later
decisions on those diverged trajectories are dependent consequences, not
independent mechanism activations. An independent old-screen activation audit
is recorded separately; it supplies mechanism and host cost evidence rather
than new strength games.

The frozen mechanism clears only its own persistent goal array once after the
first exact hard elimination of all eight public template components. It
preserves ordinary goal persistence on other turns, capture evaluation, missions,
danger, history counters and RNG. Qualification covers controlled state, both
colors, one native decision per turn and concealed-rank invariance. Source and
runtime provenance, full Python/native replay and Qt6 review are retained.

This is development move-policy evidence; Fortress deployment choice is not
measured on forced armies. Neither validation partition was used, and no gate,
coefficient or policy source was tuned from these outcomes. Production remains
unchanged. Cycle 108 is the separate DOS endpoint.
