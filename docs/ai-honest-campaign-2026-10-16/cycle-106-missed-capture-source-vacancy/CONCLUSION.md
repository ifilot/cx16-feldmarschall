# Source-vacancy missed-capture evidence: DOS screen

The geometry-corrected missed-capture model did **not establish a DOS gain**.
It scored **39.57%** (76 wins, 49 draws, 129 losses; two unscored errors), versus
**39.80%** for the unchanged Fortress-template reference (77 wins, 49 draws,
129 losses; one error). Among the 126 paired records complete in both arms, the
candidate-minus-reference difference was **−0.40 percentage points**, 95%
bootstrap interval **[−1.19, 0.00]**.

Only **one complete paired score changed**, worsening for the candidate; 125
tied. The sparse bootstrap endpoint at zero does not establish a stable
regression or equivalence. All 128 planned pairs remain in the separate finite
missing-score sensitivity calculation: bounds **[−0.78, +0.39] points**, which
are not confidence intervals. Three engine-error attempts retain their saved
legal prefixes and remain unscored.

The [trajectory diagnostic](divergence.json) found six changed sequences among
254 matched games complete in both arms; five retained their outcomes. One
additional game diverged before a candidate-only engine error, and one had an
identical error prefix in both arms. All seven observed first different moves
were by the candidate at an identical preceding position. Later differences are
dependent consequences, not additional independent activations.

V2 evaluates hypothetical recapture geometry after the candidate enemy source
vacates. This prevents treating an opened own Scout recapture ray as an
unprotected opportunity. The exact single-observation conditioning, one-half
likelihood, event lifetime, prior and native consumer remain unchanged. The
behavioral likelihood is a fixed approximation, not a deduction that the enemy
could not win the capture. A moved clue can still occupy the single observation
slot while having no effect on the Flag/Bomb-only consumer under a background-only
posterior; that preserved scope limit is documented separately.

The change was motivated by independent mechanical review before C105 outcomes.
V1's fixed sample and qualification remain preserved. **C105 and C106 use
different cohorts**, so their raw scores do not isolate the causal effect of
the geometry correction. Cycle 107 is the separate honest development endpoint;
it also established no gain and is not pooled with these DOS results.

The fixed 512 attempts use matched original DOS armies, identical Fortress
deployments and move seeds. Source archives, exact rebuild checks, full
Python/native replay, DOS deployment audits and Qt6 captures are retained. No
likelihood, eligibility gate or sample size was tuned from these outcomes.
Production remains unchanged; no CX16 fit claim follows from desktop play.
