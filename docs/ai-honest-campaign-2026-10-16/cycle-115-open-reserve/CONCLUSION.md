# Open reserve against original DOS

The single-swap open reserve did not establish a playing gain. It scored
38.51% (70 wins, 51 draws, 127 losses; eight unscored errors), compared with
43.06% for the frozen Fortress reference (79 wins, 59 draws, 114 losses; four
unscored errors). Both arms attempted 256 games. Score counts a draw as half a win.

The primary comparison uses 118 complete side-swapped pairs: candidate minus
reference was −4.87 percentage points, with the prespecified paired 95% interval
[−11.23, +1.48]. Thirty-four pairs improved, 50 worsened and 34 tied. This is an
exploratory screen, with neither benefit nor population equivalence established.
Across all 128 scheduled pairs, assigning every engine error any score in [0,1]
gives finite-cohort effect bounds [−6.64, −1.95] points. Those bounds show that
missing results cannot reverse the observed cohort direction; they are not a
population confidence interval. All 12 errors remain saved and unscored.

by swapping it with the outer Bomb at28. Component geometry raises potential
mobile front access from32/33 to33/33 after friendly corridors clear. The inner
Bomb shell, Flag, Spy, central Miner guard, move policy, inference and RNG stream
retain their original definitions. More accessible material did not establish
higher end-to-end strength here. The separately frozen honest Missions screen
(C116) also found no gain; these are different endpoints and are not pooled.
