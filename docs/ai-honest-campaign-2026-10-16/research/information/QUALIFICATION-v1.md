# Qualification of missed-background v1

The v1 policy and its launched fixed DOS cohort remain frozen. Complete the
registered sample and retain its results rather than silently replacing the
runtime after discovering a limitation.

Its observation means that an unknown enemy remained adjacent to our disclosed
valuable soldier while choosing a quiet move elsewhere. It is a soft behavioral
approximation, not proof that the foregone capture was safe or mandatory. The
one-half likelihood never eliminates a rank and does not guarantee a free
capture.

The v1 protection gate checks original occupancy. An enemy token can block a
Scout recapture ray and then vacate that square to make the hypothetical
capture. Specifically, Scout45, enemy55, victim65 initially blocks Scout45→65;
after enemy55→65 the ray opens. V1 can therefore describe this opportunity as
unprotected even though there is a geometrical reply. Both colors have the same
limitation. This is neither an information leak nor a count-conditioning error.

V2 has a dedicated immutable implementation in
`tools/new_campaign_information_background_v2.py`. It checks each candidate's
recapture rays on occupancy after that enemy source vacates and the attacker
occupies the victim square. It inherits the exact same likelihood, uniform
background conditioning, source-template model, event lifetime, deterministic
candidate order, deployment and native evaluator. The correction has both-color
fixtures, including the newly opened Scout ray, a remaining blocking piece,
actor exclusion, shuttle exclusion and hidden-victim exclusion.

Register v2 separately before its own fixed fresh-seed tournament. V1 versus
baseline and v2 versus baseline on distinct cohorts do not isolate the causal
effect of the geometry correction; that requires a separate prespecified common
cohort. No v1 result is renamed or silently attributed to v2.
