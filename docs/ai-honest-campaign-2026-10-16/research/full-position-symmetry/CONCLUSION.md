# Fresh public full-position horizontal symmetry control

The frozen evaluator chooses a move that differs from the reflected original choice at 125 of 356 old development roots (35.1%). This is a fresh-memory implementation component result, not a strength estimate or a persistent-policy symmetry result.

The prospective protocol uses the same 48 old games, 176 Red roots and 180 Blue roots as the earlier objectives preflight. The original history bot follows every public forced prefix. Each diagnostic position then resets native AI memory and receives only the public board (own ranks, disclosed enemy ranks, concealed enemy sentinel), enemy lost counts, shuttle history and exact public Flag/Bomb packet. The full position and both once-quantized probability columns are reflected horizontally, with the same army and seed. No hidden enemy ranks, outcomes, current screen or validation histories enter the evaluator. Setup/posterior generation itself is outside this supplied-packet control.

Both orientations match the unmodified frozen observer in all 712 choices. The temporary traced observer is compiled from the registered archive with only AI_TRACE added; archive hash and build command are retained. No frozen source or runtime was changed. A reused process receives the native position command, which calls ai_reset, before each root and orientation; it does not inherit goal/memory history between evaluations.

At 17 of the 125 differing choices, the reflected original move and mirrored selected move have equal final scores in the mirrored evaluator. At the other 108 they have different final scores. Across all 8,817 corresponding candidate moves, 6,644 scores differ; every root has some score difference. These are final native scores, including random jitter. Native scoring consumes RNG in board/move scan order, and goal selection resolves equal utilities in square order. Consequently this experiment does not identify a deterministic heuristic bias or isolate move tie ordering. Equal score here means equality in the mirrored evaluator only; it does not establish equality in both orientations.

The independent referee reflects exactly: all 356 legal-move sets, all 8,174 quiet legal transitions and 240 fully disclosed synthetic combats across both armies and all mobile attacker/defender ranks pass, including shuttle state. Four additional unit tests cover reflection involution, lakes, concealed public cells, both colors, Scout rays, shuttle restrictions, quiet transitions and the combat matrix. Native candidate sets match the corresponding referee legal sets in both orientations.

This establishes that complete public-state reflection is a viable component control and that the current seeded evaluator is not horizontally equivariant under this fresh-memory protocol. It cannot attribute any own-deployment reflection benefit, establish a persistent-history effect, or support a strength-policy launch. No policy was registered, tuned or changed.
