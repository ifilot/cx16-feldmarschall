# Cycle 40: quiet-contact beliefs show no established honest-opponent gain

Quiet occupancy scored 50.89% against the honest guarded student and inferred mobile ranks scored 50.71%, compared with 48.84% for the unchanged template policy. Neither paired comparison establishes an improvement on this cohort. Token-specific mobile ranks also show no established gain beyond occupancy alone.

Each of three immutable policies played 560 Classic games: 40 side-swapped pairs in each of seven forced-army families, against the same honest reference. Armies and move seeds match across arms. The treatments change quiet approach risk using public spatial occupancy and then token-specific mobile-rank beliefs; capture scores retain their original behavior. These are movement comparisons with supplied armies, rather than policy-owned deployment tests. The source-informed opening prior is evaluated outside its original DOS population here; the independent DOS context remains separate.

Template results were 267 wins, 13 draws and 280 losses; occupancy 281 wins, 8 draws and 271 losses; mobile ranks 279 wins, 10 draws and 271 losses. All 1,680 games completed without errors, with all 280 pairs retained for every contrast. Missing-outcome bounds collapse to the observed cohort differences and are not confidence intervals. Occupancy minus template had 52 positive, 47 negative and 181 tied pair differences (exact two-sided sign p=0.688); mobile minus occupancy had 18 positive, 17 negative and 245 tied (p=1). These diagnose pair direction, rather than formally testing the mean effect. Null intervals do not establish equivalence or the absence of any useful effect.

Batch execution was 75.95 seconds for template, 78.68 for occupancy and 76.66 for mobile ranks. Different game lengths and concurrent load prevent interpreting these as controlled per-decision timings. The tested policies are desktop Python/native drivers; this match does not establish a CX16 port or target cost. No source, model, policy ID or production behavior changed, and no tuning used this cohort.
