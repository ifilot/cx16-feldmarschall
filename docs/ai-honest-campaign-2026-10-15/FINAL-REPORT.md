# Eight-hour honest Classic AI investigation

The campaign produced 37 completed, audited cycles containing 85,160 unique
saved attempts. All launched fixed samples are settled. There are 2,169 retained
unscored engine errors, 36 verified immutable source archives, four preserved
preflight rejections and seven unrun plans. No shared control was counted twice.
The research interval is 2026-10-15 20:44:38 through 2026-10-16 04:44:38 UTC;
the final clock and requirement reconciliation are recorded in the
[completion checklist](research/finalization-v1/FINAL-AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md).

## Confirmed findings

Public formation inference and deployment remain the strongest confirmed
directions against the original-DOS setup population. The unchanged source prior
passed [fresh validation](cycle-26-template-native-validation/CONCLUSION.md)
with a paired gain of 19.32 percentage points over its native control
(95% interval [15.28, 23.36]). Adding Fortress deployment to the inference policy
passed [independent validation](cycle-41-fortress-template-validation/CONCLUSION.md):
44.33% score versus 39.54%, a paired gain of 4.98 points [3.25, 6.76]. These
are matched comparisons within their respective studies, not additive effects.
Score counts a draw as half a win. Neither result establishes a 50/50 DOS match.

The cheap compact prior also passed [fresh DOS validation](cycle-46-compact-backrow-independent-validation/CONCLUSION.md):
33.92% versus 16.67%, a paired gain of 17.83 points [13.78, 21.76]. Its
[honest-reference test](cycle-32-compact-backrow-honest-generality/CONCLUSION.md)
instead scored 43.04% versus 50.00%, with the planned wider interval entirely
negative. Source-specific success therefore cannot be sold as general strength.
Richer procedural backgrounds likewise lost strength on unfamiliar supplied
armies in [C30](cycle-30-procedural-background-honest-generality/CONCLUSION.md).

There is separate fair-opponent progress: Fortress deployment passed
[honest-reference validation](cycle-25-fortress-honest-validation/CONCLUSION.md),
and exact contact risk scored 54.32% against its honest guard in
[C17](cycle-17-honest-head-validation/CONCLUSION.md), with the three-endpoint
98.3333% interval above 50%. The two teacher endpoints there were inconclusive.
The same contact idea did not establish a DOS gain in
[C14](cycle-14-native-contact-validation/CONCLUSION.md). Opponents and setup
contexts remain separate questions.

## Promising, rejected and engineering results

Quiet mobile-rank reasoning beat occupancy alone in [C39](cycle-39-quiet-contact-dos-screen/CONCLUSION.md),
but improvement over the unchanged book remained inconclusive under the planned
three-comparison interval. The [honest comparison](cycle-40-quiet-contact-honest-generality/CONCLUSION.md)
established no gain. Public forks established no gain against
[DOS](cycle-43-public-forks-dos-screen/CONCLUSION.md) or an
[honest reference](cycle-44-public-forks-honest-generality/CONCLUSION.md);
[capture nets](cycle-48-capture-net-honest-generality/CONCLUSION.md) also remained
inconclusive. Sparse changed pair scores do not establish policy equivalence.

The [native Q24 screen](cycle-42-template-q24-native-dos-screen/CONCLUSION.md)
retained the source prior's advantage. Its [exact cache bridge](cycle-45-template-exact-cache-bridge/CONCLUSION.md)
matched all 400 complete trajectories and error prefixes across 800 attempts.
Cache-hit target fixtures are encouraging, but the experimental bank recipe,
cache-miss latency, memory limits and whole-turn costs still require engineering.
Default production behavior remains unchanged.

The preferred next strategic hypothesis is using existing mobile-rank beliefs
for long-range goal selection. A fixed component pilot found concrete pursuit
changes, but no new policy was played. Storage reuse and bounded exact arithmetic
now have source-bound preflights. Implementation, both-color mechanical tests,
screening and fresh validation remain future work. See
[next steps](HONEST-NEXT-STEPS.md). No honest-AI ceiling has been established.
