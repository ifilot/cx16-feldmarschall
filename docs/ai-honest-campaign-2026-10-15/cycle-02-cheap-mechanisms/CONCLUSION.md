# Cycle 2: three cheap mechanisms yield no reliable gain

Each arm attempted 420 matched Classic games against DOS. The original Context
Teacher scored 28.5%, Latin-hypercube sampling 27.5%, army activation 27.8%, and
paired uncertainty control 29.0%. There were 1,680 attempts and 33 original DOS errors,
retained unscored.

Matched score differences from the baseline were −0.4 percentage points for
Latin sampling (95% interval −5.0 to +4.1), −0.8 for activation (−5.5 to +4.1), and
+0.5 for uncertainty control (−3.4 to +4.5). Multiplicity-adjusted 98.333% intervals also
cross zero. None supports a promotion. These are inconclusive strength effects,
not proof that every mechanism is harmful or useless in other architectures.

Activation increased drawn games from 6 to 14 without reducing the 291 losses; its
higher geometric mobility is not evidence of better strategic progress. The
one-standard-error selector's small point lead does not justify tuning its
penalty on these seeds. Preserve all versions and outcomes. Production stays
unchanged. Proceed to distinctly different tactical-horizon, public-memory,
public-deduction and correlated-belief hypotheses.
