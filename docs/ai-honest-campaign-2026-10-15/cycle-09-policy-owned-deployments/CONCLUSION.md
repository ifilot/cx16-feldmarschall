Changing the deployment mixture looks promising for the native student, but this cycle does not establish a reliable general improvement. The same deployment change did not improve the Context Teacher. Keep both experimental policies and their evidence; production remains unchanged.

Each honest policy chose its own army through its frozen setup protocol, without receiving an enemy army. Its move evaluator stayed the same. DOS received armies from seven stress families: fortified, uniform, unguarded, forward, decoy, presets and broad-mixed. Each policy attempted 420 games, with paired colors and matched DOS armies and move seeds across comparison arms. This measures setup selection plus subsequent play against those stress deployments. It does not measure the default original DOS deployment distribution or its default difficulty. This cycle used the original `policy-owned` side-swap convention, which carries the same canonical setup strings across colors; it predates the separate mode that physically rotates both armies 180 degrees.

The campaign's wider 98.33% intervals also include zero: [−1.6, +13.4] points for the native student and [−8.9, +6.5] for the teacher. These are exploratory campaign comparisons, not fresh confirmation of a selected winner.

Engine errors remain unscored: the native candidate had 16 against its baseline's 9; the teacher candidate had 28 against its baseline's 17. The sensitivity bounds retain all 210 planned rounds in each comparison and assign unknown outcomes their most favorable or unfavorable values. They are finite-cohort bounds, not confidence intervals. The native candidate stays ahead within this particular planned cohort even under adverse assignments, while the teacher's direction remains unresolved. That does not remove uncertainty about performance on new seeds or a different opponent deployment distribution.

Family effects vary substantially. Native paired gains range from −3.4 points on broad-mixed and −2.5 on decoy to +12.0 on fortified and +18.1 on presets; only 18 preset rounds were complete. Teacher effects range from −11.3 on unguarded and −5.8 on forward to +9.2 on decoy. A single average therefore hides useful differences between deployments, and the native result should not be described as a universal setup advantage.
